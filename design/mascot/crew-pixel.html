Tinkr and the ghost crew

The star reads what you asked your AI to build and sends the crew. Each ghost checks your app a different way and reports back in plain words. Switch the moment below, and click any of them to boop it.

Every sprite is a 40 × 40 pixel drawing scaled up with crisp edges, and motion moves in whole steps so it stays pixel-sharp. One dark outline and one shading rule for the whole crew, so they read as a set. A ghost that finds a problem gets a pink flag, never red. This sheet replaces the light version as the reference for @tinkr/star.
