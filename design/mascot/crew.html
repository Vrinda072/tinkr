Tinkr and the ghost crew

The star reads your prompts and sends the crew. Each ghost checks your app a different way, then reports back in plain words. Switch the moment below to see how they react, and click any of them to boop it.

Same ink, same plum outline and same face language as the approved Butter Bun star, so the six read as one crew. Body tints match the report colours sparingly; a ghost that finds a problem never turns red. When a promise is kept, the ghosts lose: they slump and go dizzy, and the star celebrates. This page is the visual reference Kiro builds the @tinkr/star package from.
