Tell your table what to do.

Type a sentence like “put red between blue and green.” Tinkr turns it into a rule, watches real objects through your webcam, and shows you the moment it’s true.

Works with coloured blocks or paper squares and any webcam. Runs in your browser.

“Put red between blue and green.”Compiled to BETWEEN(red, blue, green) and checked live

How Tinkr decides

  1. You say it

    Describe where things should go, in your own words.

  2. Tinkr writes the rule

    A language model turns the sentence into a small, strict rule. Anything it can’t express is rejected, not guessed.

    { "type": "BETWEEN", "subject": "red", "a": "blue", "b": "green" }

  3. The camera watches

    Each coloured object is tracked in your browser, about 15 times a second. Frames never leave your computer.

  4. Code decides

    A deterministic rule engine checks the rule against what the camera sees. If it can’t see something, it says so.

Two apps, one engine

Tinkr Quest

Physical puzzles for your desk. Each level is a sentence; solve it by moving real blocks. The star cheers when you get it.

Tinkr Inspect

Describe an assembly in plain steps. Tinkr checks each part is placed, in the right order, and writes a report.

  • PassedRed part in the assembly area
  • MisplacedBlue part next to red
  • WaitingGreen part between red and blue

The language model only writes the rule. Whether a rule is met is decided by open-source code you can read and test.
